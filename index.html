Our Core Capabilities

One Reasoning Loop. Every Visitor Complaint.

Heritage Signal OS connects emails, surveys, reviews, social media, ticket-desk notes and accessibility reports into one closed-loop operational layer diagnosing root causes before they become repeat failures, not after.

Signal Graph
Module 01

Heritage Operations Signal Graph

Connects visitor complaints to journey stages, locations, ticket types, visitor segments and operational teams.

Complaints about arrival delays, confusing directions and school-group congestion may look unrelated until they're mapped onto the same operational signal graph revealing a single broken arrival process.
Reasoning Engine
Module 02

Journey-Cause Reasoning Engine

Analyses complaint language alongside operational context staffing, events, ticketing, exhibitions to find true root causes.

Rather than labelling a complaint "queueing", the engine asks why: unclear signage, staffing shortages or membership-validation delays and recommends the fix that addresses the actual cause.
Access Risk
Module 03

Hidden Accessibility Risk Detector

Detects implicit accessibility and inclusion issues even when visitors never use the word "accessibility".

Phrases such as "couldn't find where to go" or "too crowded to access the exhibition" are flagged as potential physical, sensory or cognitive access barriers surfacing risks that routine complaint logs miss.
Friction DNA
Module 04

Friction DNA Profiler

Scores every complaint pattern across emotional intensity, accessibility impact, safety, revenue and reputational risk.

This lets venue managers prioritise by real-world impact rather than complaint volume alone so a low-frequency accessibility barrier gets the same attention as a high-volume queueing issue.
Memory Loop
Module 05

Intervention Memory Loop

Tracks the corrective actions a venue takes and measures whether related complaints actually fall over time.

Successful interventions strengthen future recommendations; ineffective ones trigger the system to reassess root causes and propose alternative fixes a continuously learning operational memory.
Benchmarking
Module 06

Anonymised Heritage Benchmark Layer

Compares complaint patterns, friction trends and accessibility performance anonymously across participating venues.

As more institutions join, the benchmarking layer becomes a sector-wide intelligence network giving every venue access to collective insight unavailable elsewhere, without exposing private data.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What problem does Heritage Signal OS actually solve?

+
Museums and heritage venues receive feedback across emails, surveys, reviews, social media and front-of-house logs, but rarely convert it into structured operational intelligence. Heritage Signal OS fuses these channels and identifies root causes, accessibility risks and recommended interventions not just complaint counts.

Do we need to replace our existing CRM or ticketing system?

+
No. Heritage Signal OS sits above your existing stack CRM, helpdesk, survey and review platforms ingesting and enriching that data with heritage-specific reasoning rather than replacing it.

How is this different from a survey tool or BI dashboard?

+
Survey tools and BI dashboards show what visitors are saying. Heritage Signal OS explains why an issue is happening, where in the visitor journey it occurs, what action should be taken, and whether that action actually worked a closed reasoning loop, not a static report.

How does the platform detect accessibility risks?

+
The Hidden Accessibility Risk Detector recognises indicators of physical, sensory, cognitive, communication and digital barriers in ordinary complaint language even when visitors never explicitly mention disability or access issues.

Is the platform UK GDPR compliant?

+
Yes. The platform is built around UK GDPR requirements, with encrypted data storage, secure cloud infrastructure, role-based access controls, audit logging and anonymisation processes for benchmarking activity.

What results does the plan target?

+
The plan targets a 15–25% average complaint reduction by Year 2, rising to 20–30% repeat-complaint reduction by Year 3, alongside ≥90% customer retention as the venue base scales toward ≈51 paying organisations.
